    Social content · Padel Finder

    Finding a court is easy. Finding the 4th player is the real sport.

    1 Oct31 Oct

      Shoot list

      Reels, on court

      Padel Finder × The TriFactory

      On the ground

      Event
      Marakez Pyramids Half Marathon by The TriFactory
      Race day
      Saturday, 13 Dec 2025
      Race expo
      Mall of Arabia, El Sheikh ZayedThu 11 Dec, 14:00–20:00 · Fri 12 Dec, 12:00–20:00

      Objectives

      1. Drive app downloads and registrations (main KPI)
      2. Increase brand visibility
      3. Create shareable, memorable moments for participants

      Zone setup

      • Branded booth. PF backdrop, flags and QR codes to download the app.
      • Volunteers in PF t-shirts to guide visitors, hand out prizes and run games.
      • Download first. To join any game, visitors download and register on the spot.
      01

      Spin the wheel

      Free padel bookings, water bottles, stickers and discount codes.

      02

      Photobooth

      Branded paddles, rackets and quote props like “Game. Set. Match.” Instant printed copy.

      03

      Snap. Share. Score.

      Post a booth photo tagging @PadelFinder. Most engagement wins 5,000 EGP or a Samsung wristband.

      04

      Serve & Win

      Serve a ball through a wooden board with different-sized holes. Each hole is a different prize.

      05

      Challenge a Friend

      A mini padbol half court. Pair up and rally, no skill needed, then take home “Your First Padbol Rally.”

      06

      2-Minute Juggle

      Keep a football in the air for two minutes without it touching the ground.

      07

      Confessional Booth

      Record a 15-second sports confession. The funniest get featured on PF socials after the event.

      08

      Your First Match in VR

      VR goggles, a virtual court and a 60–90 second rally against an AI opponent or a friend.

      Constraints

      Space & power. Socket count, load and cable management pre-cleared with expo ops.

      Queues. Friday is peak pre-race traffic, so every game takes 60 seconds or less and stays low-sweat.
